A private practice, authority and guidance

Imagine this situation

You belong to a Christian community that matters to you. It announces a rule: members must avoid a certain ordinary food on one day each week, even when eating alone.

Nobody observes or checks what you eat. For this exercise, assume Scripture does not directly command or forbid eating this food on that day. You need not assume that the community has the right to make the rule.

Draft experiment, not a tradition-matching test. These questions do not ask whether you have actually received guidance. All paths are predefined. Answers remain on this page and are not sent or stored.
