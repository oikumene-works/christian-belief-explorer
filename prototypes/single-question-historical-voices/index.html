Christian Belief Explorer · experimental single-question unit

One question, three voices from Christian history

Answer one question in your own way. Afterward, you will see how three Christian thinkers from different centuries approached the same issue. There are no scores and no tradition label.

Your question

Suppose you have studied a Bible passage carefully and still think your church explains it wrongly. Could there ever be a case where God expects you to accept your church’s explanation as right because the church has the responsibility to teach its members?

What “accept as right” means here: believing that you ought to affirm the church’s explanation—not merely following a shared rule, keeping quiet, or listening seriously to the church’s reasons.

Three voices from Christian history

Everyone sees the same three voices. Your answer changes only the order and the short comparison text. Agreement on this one question does not imply agreement in theology as a whole.

These three people do not represent every Christian answer. Each card describes one bounded text or event, not the person’s entire theology.
